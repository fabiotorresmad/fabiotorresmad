## 👨‍💻 About Me

Electronic Engineer with 4+ years of industrial experience designing, implementing, and validating firmware and embedded Linux systems. My background spans:

- 🏥 **Safety-Critical Medical Firmware:** Developing multicore ARM firmware, inter-processor communication (IPC) protocols, and distributed architecture for mechanical ventilators at **Intermed**.
- 🏢 **Access Control & Telemetry Systems:** Designing deterministic, low-latency firmware in C and hardware validation routines at **DIMEP**.
- 🏭 **Industrial IoT Gateways & Sensor Networks:** Engineered multi-protocol edge gateways running on Embedded Linux (Raspberry Pi), bridging industrial buses (Modbus RTU/TCP, BACnet) and 6LoWPAN wireless mesh networks (Contiki) to cloud platforms at **Omni-Electronica**.
- 🎓 **Academic Research:** Master’s candidate in Electrical Engineering (Electronic Systems) at the **University of São Paulo (USP)**, focusing on **V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything)** communication, Connected Autonomous Vehicles, and Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
